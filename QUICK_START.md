## 💡 Customization Tips

### 1. Add Your Institution Details
Replace in main document:
```latex
\author{Your Name\\Your Institution}
```
With:
```latex
\author{Ariyan Bhakat\\[Your University Name]\\Department of [Your Department]}
```

### 2. Adjust Budget to Your Resources
If you have more/less funding, modify the budget section:
- More funding → Add more IoT sensors, better devices
- Less funding → Use simulation instead of hardware, free APIs

### 3. Modify Timeline
Adjust phase timelines based on your practicum duration:
- 6-month practicum → Choose Project 2 (5-7 months)
- 12-month practicum → Any project with extended validation phase

### 4. Add Local Context
In the problem statement, add local examples:
- "Our university consumes X kWh annually..."
- "Y% of students in our region have visual impairments..."
- "Local farmers lose Z% to intermediaries..."

## 🎓 Key Talking Points for Your Pitch

### Opening Hook (Choose one based on project):
1. **Energy:** "US universities spend $14 billion annually on energy—25-40% of which is completely wasted. What if AI could cut that in half while protecting student privacy?"

2. **Accessibility:** "2.2 billion people live with vision impairment. Existing solutions cost $4,500. What if a smartphone app using GPT-4V could provide the same assistance for free?"

3. **Agriculture:** "Farmers receive only 10% of retail prices while consumers can't verify food safety. What if blockchain + digital twins could solve both problems simultaneously?"

### Gap Emphasis:
Always say: **"Based on 2025-2026 research, I identified 5 critical gaps that no current solution addresses..."**

Then list your gaps with citations:
- "ArXiv 2026 found existing apps are 'limited to advanced users only'"
- "Frontiers 2026 reported only 3% achieve full integration"
- "NIH 2026 showed multimodal feedback improves accuracy by 40%"

### Innovation Statement:
"Our innovation is the **first** to combine [Tech A] + [Tech B] + [Tech C] to address [Problem]."

Examples:
- "First privacy-preserving campus energy system using federated learning"
- "First unified accessibility platform combining GPT-4V, haptics, and spatial audio"
- "First agricultural blockchain integrating SSI, digital twins, and edge computing"

## 📊 Expected Questions & Answers

### Q: "Why hasn't someone built this already?"
**A:** "Great question! These technologies are very recent:
- GPT-4V launched March 2023
- Federated learning frameworks matured in 2024-2025
- Self-sovereign identity standards finalized in 2025

The research gap I identified is **integration**—these technologies exist separately but haven't been combined for this application."

### Q: "How do you know this will work?"
**A:** "I'm basing this on validated research:
- [Cite specific paper] showed [X% improvement]
- [Cite specific paper] demonstrated [Y outcome]
- My innovation applies these proven techniques to a new domain"

### Q: "What if you can't get access to [campus/farmers/users]?"
**A:** "I have a fallback plan:
- Simulation using synthetic data
- Partnership with [organization name]
- Scaled-down proof-of-concept with [alternative]"

### Q: "What's your biggest risk?"
**A:** "I've identified 3-4 risks in my proposal with specific mitigation strategies:
- [Risk 1] → [Mitigation]
- [Risk 2] → [Mitigation]
The most critical is [X], which I'll address by [Y]"
