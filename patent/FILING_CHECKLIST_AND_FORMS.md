## 4. STEP-BY-STEP E-FILING GUIDE

### 4.1 Filing in India via IPO InPASS Portal:
1. Log in to the official portal: [https://ipindiaonline.gov.in/epatentfiling/](https://ipindiaonline.gov.in/epatentfiling/) with Class 3 Digital Signature Certificate (DSC).
2. Click **New Application** $\rightarrow$ Select **Ordinary Application** with **Complete Specification**.
3. Upload Form 1, Form 2 (combining Specification, Claims, Abstract, Drawings), Form 3, and Form 5.
4. Pay the statutory filing fee (₹1,600 for Individual / Startup; ₹8,000 for Large Entity).
5. (Optional but Recommended) File **Form 9** for Early Publication (within 1 month instead of waiting 18 months).
6. File **Form 18** (Request for Examination) within 48 months (or Form 18A for Expedited Examination if eligible as a recognized DPIIT Startup).

### 4.2 Filing in the US via USPTO Patent Center:
1. Access the USPTO Patent Center: [https://patentcenter.uspto.gov/](https://patentcenter.uspto.gov/) using your USPTO My.gov credentials.
2. Select **File Nonprovisional Utility Application**.
3. Upload PDF documents:
   - Specification (with Abstract)
   - Claims
   - Formal Drawings
   - Application Data Sheet (ADS)
4. Pay the Basic Filing, Search, and Examination Fees (Micro Entity discount applies if eligible).
5. Receive the electronic filing receipt with the Application Serial Number and Filing Date.

### 4.3 International PCT Filing Route:
- Within **12 months** from the priority filing date (India or US), submit a PCT international application with the Indian Patent Office or WIPO as the Receiving Office (RO).
- Designate all PCT member states (157+ countries).
- Enter the National Phase in target jurisdictions (US, Europe/EPO, Japan/JPO, etc.) at month 30 or 31.
